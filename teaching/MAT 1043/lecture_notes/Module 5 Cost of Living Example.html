Module 5: Cost of Living Example

Example

Suppose we are job hunting again and extend our search to outside of Texas, below are other job offers.

City Comparisons
Austin (AUS) New York City (NYC) Denver (DEN)
Salary Offer $70,000 $120,000 $85,000
Cost of Living $50,000 $100,000 $60,000

Computing our cost of living ratios \(\)

Austin Ratios:

\begin{align*}
\text{AUS }\to\text{NYC} &= \frac{$50,000 \text{ AUS}}{$100,000 \text{ NYC}} = \frac{0.50 \text{ AUS}}{1 \text{ NYC}} \\[2pt]
\text{AUS }\to\text{DEN} &= \frac{$50,000 \text{ AUS}}{$60,000 \text{ DEN}} = \frac{0.83 \text{ AUS}}{1 \text{ DEN}}
\end{align*}

New York City Ratios:

\begin{align*}
\text{NYC }\to\text{AUS} &= \frac{$100,000 \text{ NYC}}{$50,000 \text{ AUS}} = \frac{2 \text{ NYC}}{1 \text{ AUS}} \\[2pt]
\text{NYC }\to\text{DEN} &= \frac{$100,000 \text{ NYC}}{$60,000 \text{ DEN}} = \frac{1.67 \text{ NYC}}{1 \text{ DEN}}
\end{align*}

Denver Ratios:

\begin{align*}
\text{DEN }\to\text{AUS} &= \frac{$60,000 \text{ DEN}}{$50,000 \text{ AUS}} = \frac{1.20 \text{ DEN}}{1 \text{ AUS}} \\[2pt]
\text{DEN }\to\text{NYC} &= \frac{$60,000 \text{ DEN}}{$100,000 \text{ NYC}} = \frac{0.60 \text{ DEN}}{1 \text{ NYC}}
\end{align*}

We now build our table while noting that we already know the salary in each city.

Salary Comparison Table
AUS Salary NYC Salary DEN Salary
Value in AUS $70,000 - -
Value in NYC - $120,000 -
Value in DEN - - $85,000

Working through the dimensional analysis for each salary equivalence below filling the table left to right, top to bottom.

New York City Salary to Austin Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $120,000 \text{ NYC} & \Big| & 0.50 \text{ AUS} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ NYC}  \end{matrix}
= $60,000 \text{ AUS}\]

Denver Salary to Austin Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $85,000 \text{ DEN} & \Big| & 0.83 \text{ AUS} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ DEN}  \end{matrix}
= $70,550 \text{ AUS}\]

Austin Salary to New York City Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $70,000 \text{ AUS} & \Big| & 2 \text{ NYC} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ AUS}  \end{matrix}
= $140,000 \text{ NYC}\]

Denver Salary to New York City Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $85,000 \text{ DEN} & \Big| & 1.67 \text{ NYC} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ DEN}  \end{matrix}
= $141,950 \text{ NYC}\]

Austin Salary to Denver Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $70,000 \text{ AUS} & \Big| & 1.20 \text{ DEN} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ AUS}  \end{matrix}
= $84,000 \text{ DEN}\]

New York City Salary to Denver Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $120,000 \text{ NYC} & \Big| & 0.60 \text{ DEN} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ NYC}  \end{matrix}
= $72,000 \text{ DEN}\]

Filling in our table:

Hence, looking at the highest column, we that the Denver Salary gets us the most value for our money.
