Motivation

We want to understand how things change with time, we have started working with this concept with the weighted moving average, but now we consider a more direct mean of understanding change.

Absolute Change

Our most direct way to measure change is by simply finding the difference.

Definition (Absolute Change).

The difference measuring how much something has changed, calculated \( \text{End } - \text{ Start} \).

When taking finding the change \( B\text{-value} - A\text{-value} \), we will say 'The absolute change in \( X \) from \( A \) to \( B \), where \( X \) is some context of the data.

As an example, suppose we want to find the absolute change in housing prices in San Antonio from year to year. Below is our data

Maximum Median House Price by Year
2022 2023 2024
San Antonio $382,400 $367,325 $349,000

If we wanted to find the absolute change in San Antonio from 2022 to 2024, we would take

\[ $349,000 - $382,400 = -$33,400. \]

We would expect this to be a negative value since the prices have decreased.

If we wanted to find the absolute change in San Antonio from 2023 to 2022, we would take

\[ $382,400 - $367,325 = $15,075. \]

Relative Change

Sometimes we wish to know how much something has changed in proportion to another thing to do so we find the relative change.

Definition (Relative Change).

Also known as the percent change, a measure of increase or decrease as a percent, computed as the absolute change over the starting value, i.e.,

\[ \frac{\text{End } - \text{ Start}}{\text{Start}}. \]

Returning to our previous example

Maximum Median House Price by Year
2022 2023 2024
San Antonio $382,400 $367,325 $349,000

If we wanted to find the relative change in San Antonio from 2022 to 2024, we would take

\[\frac{349,000 - 382,400}{382,400} \approx -0.0873 \quad \text{or } -8.73\% \]

read as a 8.75% decrease.

If we wanted to find the relative change in San Antonio from 2023 to 2022, we would take

\[\frac{382,400 - 367,325}{367,325} \approx 0.0410 \quad \text{or } 4.10\% \]

a 4.10 % increase.
