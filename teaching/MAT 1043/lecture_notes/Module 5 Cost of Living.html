Motivation

We want the most bang for our buck, that is, the most out of our money. Depending on location, life is more expensive, we aim to maximize our income based on where we live.

Cost of Living Ratios

Definition (Cost of Living).

The cost of living combines all expenses of daily life including food, transport, housing, etc.

Depending on where and how we wish to live, our cost of living can differ wildly. After figuring out what type of arrangements we want relative to each location, we sum up all of our costs and find a general value we would have to pay in order to have the life we want. Since things cost different from place to place, we need to find ways of converting value from one to another.

Definition (Cost of Living Ratio).

The ratio from City \(A\) to City \(B\), a conversion unit.

We compute these ratio as follows

\[ \frac{\text{Cost of Living in City }A}{\text{Cost of Living in City }B}\]

We will record the different cost of living ratios and use them in our conversions.

Currency Conversions

Now that we have a unit conversion, we can take a salary in one location and scale it to another, finding what that salary would look like in the other location. This is done using dimensional Analysis! 

For instance, if we wanted to find the value of City \(A\) dollars in City \(B\), we would find so with the following dimensional analysis

\[ \begin{matrix} \text{Salary in City }A & \Big| & \text{Cost of Living in City }B \\
\hline & \Big| & \text{Cost of Living in City }A  \end{matrix}
= \text{ City \(A\)'s Salary Value in City B}\]

Once we have all the conversions we will compile them in a table for comparisons

Salary Comparison Table
City \( A \) Salary City \( B \) Salary City \( C \) Salary
Value in City \( A \) - - -
Value in City \( B \) - - -
Value in City \( C \) - - -

After creating our table we will look for the column with the highest values, this will tell us where we will find the best value for a given salary.

An Example

Suppose we are job hunting in Texas, we get three job offers all in different cities, then we look up a cost of living for our lifestyle in each city and record them as follows.

City Comparisons
San Antonio (SA) Dallas (DAL) Houston (HOU)
Salary Offer $65,000 $150,000 $100,000
Cost of Living $38,000 $125,000 $82,000

Computing our cost of living ratios

San Antonio Ratios:

\begin{align*}
\text{SA }\to\text{DAL} &= \frac{$38,000 \text{ SA}}{$125,000 \text{ DAL}} = \frac{0.30 \text{ SA}}{1 \text{ DAL}} \\[2pt]
\text{SA }\to\text{HOU} &= \frac{$38,000 \text{ SA}}{$82,000 \text{ HOU}} = \frac{0.46 \text{ SA}}{1 \text{ HOU}}
\end{align*}

Dallas Ratios:

\begin{align*}
\text{DAL }\to\text{SA} &= \frac{$125,000 \text{ DAL}}{$38,000 \text{ SA}} = \frac{3.29 \text{ DAL}}{1 \text{ SA}} \\[2pt]
\text{DAL }\to\text{HOU} &= \frac{$125,000 \text{ DAL}}{$82,000 \text{ HOU}} = \frac{1.52 \text{ DAL}}{1 \text{ HOU}}
\end{align*}

Houston Ratios:

\begin{align*}
\text{HOU }\to\text{SA} &= \frac{$82,000 \text{ HOU}}{$38,000 \text{ SA}} = \frac{2.16 \text{ HOU}}{1 \text{ SA}} \\[2pt]
\text{HOU }\to\text{DAL} &= \frac{$82,000 \text{ HOU}}{$125,000 \text{ DAL}} = \frac{0.66 \text{ HOU}}{1 \text{ DAL}}
\end{align*}

We now build our table while noting that we already know the salary in each city.

Salary Comparison Table
SA Salary DAL Salary HOU Salary
Value in SA $65,000 - -
Value in DAL - $150,000 -
Value in HOU - - $100,000

Working through the dimensional analysis for each salary equivalence below filling the table left to right, top to bottom.

Dallas Salary to San Antonio Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $150,000 \text{ DAL} & \Big| & 0.3 \text{ SA} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ DAL}  \end{matrix}
= $45,000 \text{ SA}\]

Houston Salary to San Antonio Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $100,000 \text{ HOU} & \Big| & 0.46 \text{ SA} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ HOU}  \end{matrix}
= $46,000 \text{ SA}\]

San Antonio Salary to Dallas Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $65,000 \text{ SA} & \Big| & 3.29 \text{ DAL} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ SA}  \end{matrix}
= $213,850 \text{ DAL}\]

Houston Salary to Dallas Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $100,000 \text{ HOU} & \Big| & 1.52 \text{ DAL} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ HOU}  \end{matrix}
= $152,000 \text{ DAL}\]

San Antonio Salary to Houston Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $65,000 \text{ SA} & \Big| & 2.16 \text{ HOU} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ SA}  \end{matrix}
= $140,400 \text{ HOU}\]

Dallas Salary to Houston Cost of Living:

\[ \begin{matrix} $150,000 \text{ DAL} & \Big| & 0.66 \text{ HOU} \\
\hline & \Big| & 1 \text{ DAL}  \end{matrix}
= $99,000 \text{ HOU}\]

Filling in our table:

Salary Comparison Table
SA Salary DAL Salary HOU Salary
Value in SA $65,000 $45,000 $46,000
Value in DAL $213,850 $150,000 $152,000
Value in HOU $140,400 $99,000 $100,000

Hence, looking at the highest column, we that the San Antonio Salary gets us the most value for our money.

Note. 

If we purely looked at the difference between Salary and Cost of Living, we wouldn't be getting the full picture, scaling works differently, especially when we consider different currencies.
