# Pedagogy
My teaching philosophy is defined by empathy and equity. I believe that every student is capable of
doing math and succeeding academically given support and with internal motivation. I am also an
advocate of the Rights of the Learner (Crystal A. Kalinec-Craig) which are (1) You Have the Right to Be
Confused, (2) You Have the Right to Claim a Mistake, (3) Have the Right to Speak, Listen and be
Heard, and (4) You Have the Right to Write, Do, and Represent Only What Makes Sense to You.
