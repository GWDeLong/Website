# No AI Statement

I am very much against the use of [generative AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_AI). I seldom use AI tools, for research purposes only, and move away from any companies and/or software which apply AI unethically.

For transparency, I employ the Reported Engagement with AI Level or [REAL Rating](https://www.realgoodai.org/real-rating) for short, such signifiers are below.

## Recommended Reading

The [American Mathematics Society](https://www.ams.org/home/page) has compiled [a great list of resources](https://www.ams.org/ai/ai), some of which I have duplicated in the [Some Materials](#some-materials) section of this page.

Below is an excerpt from [The University of Warwick's AI policy][WMI] that caught my eye.
> In addition, when it comes to AI and deciding if you want to use it, it is worth being aware of the following:
>
> - The environmental impact of using AI is huge - in 2024, [the Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2024/09/18/energy-ai-use-electricity-water-data-centers/) found that having ChatGPT generate one 100-word email used around 500ml of water, and enough electricity to power 14 light bulbs for an hour.
> - AI data centres also continue to use large amounts of energy - something that will only continue to grow, as shown by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/new-research-energy-electricity-artificial-intelligence-ai/) in 2025.
> - A [Microsoft study from 2025](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/lee_2025_ai_critical_thinking_survey.pdf) found that using Generative AI inhibits your critical thinking and problem solving skills. This was backed up with similar findings in a [subsequent study at MIT](https://www.media.mit.edu/publications/your-brain-on-chatgpt/) looking at the use of AI to help write essays.

## Recommended Tools

Most of these tools, all of which are free, are found in the [Better Internet Guide][Deshittification] below, however I enjoy lists.

- Operating System: [Zorin](https://zorin.com) or any [Linux distribution](https://www.linux.org/pages/download/)
- Browser and Search Engine: [Vivaldi](https://vivaldi.com) with [Duck Duck Go](https://noai.duckduckgo.com) ([No-AI Extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/duckduckgo-no-ai-search/faoilnlkccdjdkpljainiiimmijofmpd))
- File Archiver: [7-Zip](https://7-zip.org)
- Office Products: [LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org)
- Note Taking: [Xournal++](https://xournalpp.github.io)
- TeX Editing: [TeXstudio](https://texstudio.org)
- Video Player: [VLC Media Player](https://www.videolan.org)
- Image Editing: [GNU Image Manipulation Program (GIMP)](https://www.gimp.org)
- Video Editing: [Kdenlive](https://kdenlive.org)
- Audio Editing: [Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org)
